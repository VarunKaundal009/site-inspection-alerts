B10 city inspection alerts

Get a notification on your phone when a B10 inspection date or time changes.

  1. Install the ntfy app

    It's free. Skip this step if ntfy is already on your phone.

    Get ntfy on the App Store Get ntfy on Google Play

  2. Turn on B10 alerts

    Opens ntfy and adds the B10 inspection channel. Tap Subscribe if asked, then allow notifications.

    Open in ntfy

    If the button doesn't open ntfy, open the app, tap +, and enter this channel name:
