City inspection alerts

Get a notification on your phone when a city inspection date or time changes in your building.

  1. Install the ntfy app

    It's free. Skip this step if ntfy is already on your phone.

    Get ntfy on the App Store Get ntfy on Google Play

  2. Copy your building's channel

    Work in both buildings? Add both channels, one at a time.

    B9
    b9-city-insp-8f98d1
    B10
    b10-city-insp-5a558d

  3. Add the channel in ntfy

    Open the ntfy app and tap +.
    Paste the channel name and tap Subscribe.
    Allow notifications when your phone asks.
